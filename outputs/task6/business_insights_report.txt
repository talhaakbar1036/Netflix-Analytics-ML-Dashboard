4. BUSINESS RECOMMENDATIONS
----------------------------------------
1. Content mix: Movies dominate the catalogue (70%). Given TV Shows drive longer subscriber engagement/retention industry-wide, consider increasing TV Show investment, especially in high-performing markets.

2. Market focus: United States and India lead content production. Underrepresented but populous markets could be prioritized for regional originals to diversify the catalogue and reduce single-market dependency.

3. Catalogue growth: the forecast model projects roughly 1116 new titles/year by 2025 if historical trends continue; treat this as a baseline for content-budget planning, not a guarantee, since 2020-2021 already broke the historical growth pattern.

4. Ratings strategy: 'TV-MA' is the most common content rating, indicating a mature-audience skew; family-friendly ('TV-G', 'TV-Y') content is comparatively under-represented and could support subscriber-base broadening.

5. Data quality: 29% of titles are missing director metadata. Improving metadata completeness would materially improve future recommendation-engine and analytics accuracy.
